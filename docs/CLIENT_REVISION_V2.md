# Kingfisher Park — Client Revision V2

## Hero decisions
- Removed the Mangrove Kayak price from the Hero CTA. Rates remain in Experiences and Booking where visitors compare options.
- Replaced stage-specific Hero booking labels with one stable **BOOK YOUR EXPERIENCE** CTA.
- Kept **Mangrove Kayak** as contextual Daylight content, not as the primary sitewide booking action.
- Added a clear Daylight tide advisory: availability depends on low/high tide conditions and resulting water depth.
- Reduced the oversized title and repositioned the Hero statement to prevent overlap on normal laptop screens.
- Reduced the hard 850px Hero minimum so the full Hero can fit a normal viewport at 100% browser zoom.

## Tide wording
The tide advisory was also synchronized across Experiences, Booking, Plan Your Visit and Visitor Guide.

No new tide schedule, automatic tide data or guaranteed high-tide availability was invented.
