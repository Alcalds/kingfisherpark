The client brochure was treated as source evidence, **not final copy**. New headlines and paragraphs were authored for their receiving pages and contextual constraints. Factual rates, official names, age thresholds and activities retain their source meaning.

## What remains inherited

Previously accepted V7 archival statements, biodiversity gallery labels, and HOME Lightline concepts were retained unless they conflicted with confirmed new information. The audit does not imply every inherited sentence was rewritten; it deliberately preserves accepted source content where no change was needed.

## Editorial guardrails

Do not guarantee particular wildlife sightings, dates, times or boat resources. Do not call the old ₱1,000 Complete Experience a confirmed current product. Do not display V7 individual prices as live V9 rates before staff reconfirms them. Avoid using the same client weaving photograph across unrelated sections.
