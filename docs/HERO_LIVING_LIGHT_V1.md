# KINGFISHER PARK — LIVING LIGHT HERO V1

## Purpose
Replace the standard full-screen tourism Hero with a Kingfisher Park-specific interaction.

## Interaction
The bottom Lightline changes the Hero continuously from:

1. DAYLIGHT
2. GOLDEN HOUR
3. AFTER DARK

The visitor can:
- drag or tap the range line;
- select any of the three stage labels;
- use the range input with a keyboard.

## Scene behavior
- Daylight: 2026 aerial mangrove image dominates; fireflies are effectively absent.
- Golden Hour: authentic sunset/water image blends in; a few fireflies begin to appear.
- After Dark: authentic boardwalk/night image dominates; the firefly canvas reaches full intensity.

## Dynamic CTA
- Daylight → BOOK MANGROVE KAYAK
- Golden Hour → EXPLORE EVENING EXPERIENCES
- After Dark → BOOK A NIGHT EXPERIENCE

## Authenticity
The firefly particles are clearly a decorative interface effect. They are not a representation of actual wildlife density or guaranteed sightings.

## Homepage simplification
The full biodiversity archive gallery was removed from Home and replaced by a four-image preview.
The complete gallery remains in `biodiversity.html`.

## Wix Studio feasibility
The design is intended to remain reproducible in Wix Studio using:
- overlapping image states;
- a custom slider / Velo state controller;
- the existing Canvas/custom-element firefly treatment;
- responsive typography and positioning.
