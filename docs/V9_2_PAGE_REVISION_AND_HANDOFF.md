## Reservation platform — decided, not under reconsideration

The client has **already installed Wix Bookings** in the existing site. Its native Book Online, Service Page, Booking Calendar, Booking Form, Checkout, and Thank You flow is the operational path. No mandatory member accounts. The old customized V7 demo checkout must not be treated as live, and its page should only be retired after the Wix alternative has been tested.

Remaining work is **configuration, not platform selection**: actual dates/times/capacities, precise Tour A/B adult-tier/child calculations in checkout, connected merchant provider, approved deposit policy, receipt/confirmation and staff check-in, and synchronization of Messenger-origin reservations.

## Commercial policy snapshot

- Confirmed shorter activities: Mangrove Kayak **₱600**, Firefly Boardwalk **₱400**, Fireflies + Night Kayak **₱600** per paying participant. Age-specific individual activity rates are not supplied; do not infer them from Tour A/B child policy.
- Tour A: 10+ paying adults **₱2,200/adult**. Tour B: 10+ paying adults **₱3,200/adult**; children aged 4–12 at half the applicable adult rate with meals, ages 3 and below free without meal.
- Operational capacity and safety restrictions may still limit group size even when a group-rate tier applies.
- **30% deposit or 100% upfront** is an illustrative management-review choice, not a connected live payment.
- **Early Riser:** optional, pay at park, excluded from online totals.
- **Transportation not included** in Tour A/B; tour dates and capacities must be entered by staff.
