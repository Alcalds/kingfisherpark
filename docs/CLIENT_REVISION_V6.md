# Kingfisher Park — Version 6: Regenerative Agriculture Photography

## Source of imagery
All photographs in `assets/images/regenerative-agriculture/` are optimized derivatives of the user-provided ZIP titled **Regalo ng Kilit and Kingfisher Park Regenerative Agriculture Production System-1-001.zip**. Individual filenames were opaque hashes, so visual captions describe only what appears in each picture. No crop names, measured environmental outcomes, or program schedules were inferred.

## Website changes
- **The Park**: New photography-led **Growing with the landscape** section between Conservation & Community and The Park Through Time. Four photos show aerial garden plots, planting/fieldwork, growing produce, and young crops.
- **Stories & History**: New **Scenes from the growing landscape** photo essay with six additional images: field plots, trellis work, planting beds, farm animals, plant care, and preparation.
- Existing V4 HOME design, Header, Footer, subpage Hero image corrections, client firefly/Pagatpat language, and eight-stage booking simulation are preserved.
- Fixed an existing broken in-page `EXPLORE THE ARCHIVE` link on The Park; it now points to `#park-history`.
- Source photos were resized and converted to WebP for faster site loading; full original 65 JPEG files and MOV were not bundled (retain the original uploaded ZIP as source archive).
