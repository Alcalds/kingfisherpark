# Photo request — Kingfisher Park website (copy-ready)

Good day!

We are updating the Kingfisher Park website and would like to request **original, high-resolution photographs taken at Kingfisher Park**, to better illustrate the visitor experiences, biodiversity and conservation/community activities.

Please share available photographs for:

1. **Biodiversity** — birds, mangrove trees (especially a clearly identified Pagatpat tree), native plants/flowers, crabs, insects, and other wildlife photographed at the park. Please include the species name if verified, and a brief note on where it was taken. *Highest priority: more plant and non-bird wildlife photos, as the current gallery is mostly birds.*
2. **Actual visitor experiences** — daytime mangrove kayaking, Firefly Boardwalk tours, night kayaking, guides with visitors, and sunset conditions. Please use **real visitor photos** with appropriate consent; no staged handling of wildlife or flash photography around fireflies.
3. **Plan Your Visit / Visitor Guide** — current entrance and park signage, arrival/meeting point, pathways and boardwalk, orientation or guide briefing, and existing visitor facilities. Please label any facilities shown to avoid misunderstandings.
4. **The Park / Stories** — mangrove landscapes and conservation work, community activities, recent improvements, and historical photographs with dates. Current regenerative agriculture fieldwork and crops are also welcome, with short descriptions and dates.

For every image, where possible, please provide **location, approximate date, subject, photographer/credit, and permission for website publication**. Please send original JPG/PNG/HEIC photos instead of social-media screenshots, preferably at least 1600px on the longer side (1920px or more for wide Hero photographs).

A shared Google Drive folder or one ZIP file arranged by the four categories would be convenient. If some images are unavailable, please send what is currently on hand and label the missing categories.

Thank you very much for your support!
