# Kingfisher Park — Client Revision V4

## Sitewide Readability Pass

Client/staff feedback that text was too small is now applied across the entire prototype, not only the Living Light Hero.

### Updated across all pages
- Header branding and navigation
- Buttons and text links
- Section kickers and labels
- Homepage body copy and experience cards
- Landscape, biodiversity, after-dark, community, stories, visitor guidance and Plan Your Visit sections
- Subpage Hero descriptions and bridge copy
- Footer and Visitor Guide callout
- Visitor Guide page
- Mobile supporting copy and controls

### Booking flow
- Progress labels
- Step descriptions
- Experience descriptions and prices
- Form labels/help text
- Tide/status notes
- What to Expect content
- Guest controls
- Review/summary content
- Demo-payment and confirmation copy

### Design principle
Large display headlines remain editorial and dramatic. The revision specifically increases text that visitors need to read or act on, while preserving spacing and Wix Studio feasibility.

### Wix Studio compatibility
The changes are typography and spacing rules that can be recreated with Wix Studio text styles/breakpoints. No VS Code-only dependency was introduced.
