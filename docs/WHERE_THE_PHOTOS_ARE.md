# Where to find the added agriculture photographs

## The Park
1. Open `the-park.html`.
2. In the large Hero at the top, select **SEE REGENERATIVE AGRICULTURE ↘**.
3. The page jumps to **Growing with the landscape.** You will see **four photographs** in a responsive mosaic.

## Stories & History
1. Open `stories.html`.
2. In its Hero, select **VIEW AGRICULTURE PHOTO STORY ↘**.
3. The page jumps to **Scenes from the growing landscape.** You will see **six photographs** in the field-notes grid.

## Visitor Guide: not an agriculture gallery
Click **OPEN GUIDE** on any Footer/visitor guidance link. It opens `visitor-guide.html`, which includes a mangrove aerial Hero, preparation guidance, firefly protection instructions, and a Pagatpat species reference (externally hosted). This is intentional and distinct from the 10 agriculture images.

## Full image files
`assets/images/regenerative-agriculture/` has 10 optimized WebP photographs. `docs/REGENERATIVE_IMAGE_MANIFEST.json` records their corresponding originals from the uploaded ZIP. Do not confuse these images with the sitewide Hero photo assets in `assets/images/hero/`, `assets/images/night/` or `assets/images/landscape/`.

**Viewing correctly:** extract the entire ZIP first, and open `index.html` using Live Server or a local HTTP server. Relative image paths are designed to be resolved from the extracted folder, not from a ZIP preview.
