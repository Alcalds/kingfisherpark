WELCOME TO KINGFISHER PARK

Discover Coron beyond the familiar island tour.

Kingfisher Park is a nature destination in Malbato where mangrove waterways, forest paths and everyday community life come together. Experience the landscape on a short outing or discover more through a guided day in the park.

A visit might mean paddling among mangroves, seeing the forest after dark, or spending time with local guides exploring wildlife, farming, village life and food. Choose what matches your interests and the time you have.

Malbato · Coron, PalawanWaterways · Wildlife · Community
THREE WAYS TO EXPLORE

What would you like to discover?

Start with a short adventure on the water, explore the park after sunset, or discover Malbato through a full-day guided eco tour.

Paddle the mangroves

Explore quieter waterways by kayak, when tide and water depth allow.

Experience the park after dark

Take in the mangrove setting after sunset, with opportunities for firefly viewing and other nighttime wildlife experiences.

Discover Malbato in a day

Explore forest trails, local farming, community experiences and included meals on Tour A or Tour B.

Individual activities have the latest supplied per-person rates; full-day tours are priced by group size. Check details and conditions before requesting a date.

MORE THAN A NATURE VISIT

A nature visit connected to the people of Malbato.

Beyond the waterways, Kingfisher Park connects biodiversity enhancement, regenerative farming and local enterprise through BERAE. On a full-day guided tour, community and farm stops give visitors a closer look at those connections.

NatureWaterways, forests and wildlife
LearningFarming and biodiversity

A LIVING LANDSCAPE

Look closer. There is life all around.

Birdwatching, native vegetation and the mangrove habitat reward close observation. Discover the park’s photography collection and the importance of respecting wildlife without expecting guaranteed sightings.
