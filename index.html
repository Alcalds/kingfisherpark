WELCOME TO KINGFISHER PARK

Discover Coron beyond the familiar island tour.

Two distinct visits await in Malbato: Kingfisher Park Beach for guided mangrove and evening-water experiences, and the BERAE guided tours for trails, biodiversity, regenerative farming, food security and community learning.

Choose a beach activity or the Complete Experience, or make a day of the BERAE trail-and-community tour. Guides and advance scheduling help keep each visit safe and personal.

Malbato · Coron, PalawanWaterways · Wildlife · Community
TWO DISTINCT EXPERIENCES

What would you like to discover?

Kingfisher Park Beach offers four individual activities; the BERAE Tour offers a much broader guided journey through natural and working landscapes.

Paddle the mangroves

Explore the beach-side mangrove waterways by guided daytime kayak. Life jackets are required, and every kayak trip has a guide.

Experience the park after dark

Meet the shoreline after dark through the boardwalk or night kayak, with possible fireflies and bioluminescent plankton in suitable conditions.

Discover Malbato in a day

Discover the separate BERAE Tour: hillside trails, birdwatching, regenerative agriculture, community activities and food-security learning.

Beach: four individual activities, including the ₱1,000 Complete Experience. BERAE: Tour A (8 AM–5 PM) or Tour B (8 AM–7 PM), with group-based tour rates.

KINGFISHER PARK BEACH · AFTER DARK

When the water catches the light.

Bioluminescent plankton can sometimes create a fleeting glow in the water — one of the special possibilities of a night visit to Malbato.

Ask about the guided Fireflies + Night Kayak or the day-to-night Complete Experience. Visibility varies with season, tide, weather and other natural conditions; a glowing display is never guaranteed.

MORE THAN A NATURE VISIT

A nature visit connected to the people of Malbato.

Beyond the waterways, Kingfisher Park connects biodiversity enhancement, regenerative farming and local enterprise through BERAE. On a full-day guided tour, community and farm stops give visitors a closer look at those connections.

NatureWaterways, forests and wildlife
LearningFarming and biodiversity

A LIVING LANDSCAPE

Look closer. There is life all around.

Birdwatching, native vegetation and the mangrove habitat reward close observation. Discover the park’s photography collection and the importance of respecting wildlife without expecting guaranteed sightings.
