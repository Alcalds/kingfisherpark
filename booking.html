BEFORE PAYMENT

A clear booking policy.

01

Pay in full online

100% payment through the official Wix booking checkout upon confirmation. This information-only prototype never collects payments.

02

Refund cutoff

Guest-requested cancellations must be submitted at least three full days before the visit to qualify for a refund. With two days or less remaining, the payment is non-refundable.

03

Change a date

Ask park staff or Sir Tong about another date. Requests depend on staff availability, weather and session capacity.

04

Natural conditions

Guides may change or suspend paddling and viewing for safety. Wildlife and plankton displays are never guaranteed.
